## 8. Unresolved issues and access limitations

- Records still without a replacement: **0**.
- These are marked `unavailable_for_visual_analysis` in `data/quality/replacement_mapping.csv`. No placeholder records were created.
- Ad Library serves static creatives at a 600 px longest side. Higher-resolution originals are not publicly exposed, and nothing was upscaled.
- Only the public, logged-out Ad Library was used. No login, CAPTCHA or rate-limit workaround was attempted. Advertiser websites and search engines were **not** used as creative sources, because an image found there cannot be tied to a specific Ad Library ID.
